# Redline Upgrade — short film script

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Redline Upgrade is a characterful game world where every move feels like a new advantage.
- **Identity:** Use the supplied near-black / signal-orange / acid-lime palette, Barlow Condensed display type, DM Sans support, and Space Mono labels. The logo is the true brand lockup; no new logo is drawn.
- **Hero:** The official Redline Auction typography logo, three supplied character illustrations, and a graphic track that turns into a game-board route.
- **Motion:** Hard rhythmic cuts, image-plane punch-throughs, and split-panel transformations tied together by one persistent orange signal line.
- **Arc:** A fast logo ignition opens into character identity, expands into a roster, maps the world as a route, then resolves into a deliberate brand lockup.
- **Payoff:** The final shot changes from character-scale imagery to a stark oversized official lockup, then the thin route line becomes the opening logo's red strike.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.
- **Runtime:** 24 seconds, looping.

## Shot-by-shot script

### Shot 01 / 00.00–04.50 / 4.50s — “MAKE YOUR MOVE.”

**Purpose:** Immediate identity and kinetic game energy; establish the signal-line motif.

**Exact content and timing:** The supplied “REDLINE AUCTION” typography logo appears in its original artwork at 0.20s and remains legible until 3.95s. “MAKE” appears at 0.48s, “YOUR” replaces it at 1.06s, “MOVE.” replaces it at 1.64s; the three words occupy the left/center type stage at 20–25vmin. A bottom-right mono slug reads “A GAME IN MOTION” from 2.20–3.90s. No narration.

**Composition and artistic idea:** A near-black field, a razor-thin orange line at y=50%, and two oversized cropped orange-red plates create a moving shutter. The real logo is inside a horizontal lit band at center-right, never recolored. Type owns the left half; logo owns the right half. The shutters make an aperture from the exact horizontal stroke in the mark.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.20s: first red plate snaps in from top, second from bottom, with a spring-like stop. 0.20–0.48s: logo emerges through a narrow center slit while an orange line draws outward from its center. 0.48–1.80s: three word replacements punch in at 0.58s intervals; each scale overshoots briefly then settles. 1.80–2.60s: shutters retract into angled rails, exposing more of the logo. 2.60–3.55s: a single scanline sweeps over the logo; faint grid ticks move opposite direction. 3.55–4.50s: logo plate compresses to a horizontal strike which expands vertically and stays in transit into the next composition.

**Transition contract:** The outgoing horizontal strike is the next shot's divider. Combine the stripe's vertical expansion with a perspective shutter flip; the incoming Guardian image is already visible within one frame of the boundary.

**Assets and technique:** Existing local logo PNG, local Barlow Condensed / DM Sans / Space Mono fonts, CSS shutter plates, line geometry, motion transforms, restrained scanline. No generated art.

**Sound intent:** Driving electronic instrumental, no vocals. Aim the outgoing expansion at the first useful accent; beat alignment is unverified until the bed exists.

### Shot 02 / 04.50–09.00 / 4.50s — “GUARDIAN H.”

**Purpose:** Put a recognizable existing character at the heart of the experience.

**Exact content and timing:** “GUARDIAN H” appears from 4.82s through 8.65s. Small label “01 / CHARACTER FILE” at 5.30s; “HOLD THE LINE” at 6.35s. Copy is descriptive art direction, not a gameplay-stat claim.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Guardian H's supplied square portrait fills the right 58% of the frame, cropped to let the armored shoulders meet the edge; left 34% is a deep green-black title rail. The orange divider remains a vertical seam, and cyan details in the art are the only cool accent.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.45s: image resolves behind three offset horizontal shutters, scale 1.12 to 1.0. 0.45–1.30s: one shutter tracks down across the face like a scanning visor. 1.30–2.05s: “GUARDIAN H” assembles with a masked two-line reveal; the portrait punches 1.08x toward the viewer. 2.05–2.75s: the small file label traces in on a thin orange rule. 2.75–3.45s: “HOLD THE LINE” appears and then the crop pulls back slightly to include the full silhouette. 3.45–4.50s: a cyan eye-detail ring expands into three vertical windows while the title shears away.

**Transition contract:** The expanding cyan ring becomes the center window of the next shot. The orange seam splits into two matching panel edges; combine the ring aperture and a 3D card turn.

**Assets and technique:** Existing `guardian_h.png`, local fonts, crop plane and CSS shutters, SVG ring/rule. No face or costume is redrawn.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental continues; align the ring expansion with a measured beat accent if available. No VO/SFX requested.

### Shot 03 / 09.00–13.50 / 4.50s — “A ROSTER WITH ATTITUDE.”

**Purpose:** Show the range and distinctive tone of the supplied character world.

**Exact content and timing:** “A ROSTER” appears from 9.26s; “WITH ATTITUDE.” replaces it at 10.20s. Three mono captions appear under their matching panels, sequentially: “GUARDIAN H” at 9.65s, “FROSTBYTE” at 10.65s, “CLICK CLICK” at 11.65s; all remain through 12.85s.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Three tall panels of unequal widths fill the canvas: Guardian H on the left, Frostbyte center and largest, Click Click at right. The panels feel like oversized trading-card faces but retain the supplied square art intact inside each crop. Orange panel seams and lime index marks lock them into one graphic system.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.40s: cyan aperture resolves into Frostbyte's image; side cards hinge into view from shallow perspective. 0.40–1.25s: center image grows to 1.08x while left and right panels rotate into a crisp frontal lineup. 1.25–2.05s: headline words exchange on the upper-left overlay, not over the focal face. 2.05–2.90s: a narrow orange light sweep travels left-to-right across all three panels; each panel makes a 3–5 degree card tilt as the sweep passes. 2.90–3.55s: center card scales down, side cards open wider to reveal the trio. 3.55–4.50s: panel seams pull toward the bottom center and redraw as three branches of a board route.

**Transition contract:** The same orange seams visibly converge to a single route origin. The outgoing panel angles continue as the incoming path perspective; the route assembles while the card faces exit.

**Assets and technique:** Existing `guardian_h.png`, `frostbyte-player-select.png`, and `click_click.png`; CSS perspective panels, masked headline, deterministic rules and node marks. No generated characters.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental continues; three panel beats should land on three real subdivisions. No VO/SFX requested.

### Shot 04 / 13.50–18.00 / 4.50s — “EVERY MOVE OPENS A ROUTE.”

**Purpose:** Translate the project from character selection into a sense of a board game journey without depicting a false UI.

**Exact content and timing:** “EVERY MOVE” appears at 13.84s; “OPENS A ROUTE.” replaces it at 14.78s. Small tags “01”, “02”, “03” appear only beside route nodes from 14.10–17.45s.

**Composition and artistic idea:** The surface is a deep charcoal board-plane with a finite set of raised-looking square spaces, each outlined in dusty green. A signal-orange route bends from lower-left to upper-right through three lime nodes. Keep headline in top-left negative space; the route owns the lower-right diagonal.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.55s: seams from the previous shot fold flat into three short path segments; the first tile rises from the black plane. 0.55–1.45s: route draws from node 01 toward node 02 while square spaces pop up sequentially in its wake. 1.45–2.10s: the token moves node-to-node with a brief compressed landing; “EVERY MOVE” resolves in the top-left. 2.10–2.85s: route branches at node 02 and one lime branch flashes once; “OPENS A ROUTE.” replaces the headline. 2.85–3.55s: view pulls back and tilts, revealing the whole short route and a handful of connected spaces. 3.55–4.50s: final node expands into a red aperture; all route segments flow toward it, continuously carrying orange into the lockup shot.

**Transition contract:** The aperture becomes the red-lit horizontal band behind the official logo in Shot 05. Combine a pull-back with a radial aperture and let the logo plate begin arriving while the route still contracts.

**Assets and technique:** CSS/SVG track geometry and nodes only; no invented screenshot, card text, currency, or UI. Barlow Condensed title, Space Mono node labels.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental continues; route draw and aperture should sit on real percussion accents. No VO/SFX requested.

### Shot 05 / 18.00–24.00 / 6.00s — “REDLINE UPGRADE.”

**Purpose:** End on the real project identity rather than another feature demonstration.

**Exact content and timing:** “PLAY.” flashes at 18.28s; “UPGRADE.” replaces it at 18.78s; “REDLINE.” replaces it at 19.28s. The original supplied “REDLINE AUCTION” logo takes over center stage from 20.00s through 23.65s. A small label “REDLINE UPGRADE” sits beneath it from 20.40–23.65s. This is the film's title lockup, not a claim about an auction feature.

**Composition and artistic idea:** The route aperture opens onto a muted warm-ivory field that contrasts with the logo's red glow. The three punch words appear at lower-left as large black Barlow type, one at a time. Then all words clear, the original logo centers at 57% frame width with deliberate breathing room. A thin orange route line passes behind the mark without obscuring its lettering.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.45s: the final node aperture opens; logo first appears as a compressed horizontal light band. 0.45–1.65s: “PLAY.” / “UPGRADE.” / “REDLINE.” strike in at 0.50s intervals as the band unfurls; one short lime registration tick answers each word. 1.65–2.00s: words shear outward as the untouched source logo scales into its centered lockup. 2.00–3.50s: warm field settles, logo holds legibly, title resolves below. 3.50–4.70s: a faint exposure pulse and single passing red scan line keep the hold alive without distracting from the mark. 4.70–5.45s: field darkens around the logo edges and the orange route-line exits toward the exact center-height opening position. 5.45–6.00s: logo band narrows to the opening strike; next loop starts with the strike already in motion, no empty frame.

**Transition contract:** The last line's height, color, and center point match Shot 01's first strike. Blend through continuous line motion and a short exposure dip, not a blank fade.

**Assets and technique:** Existing logo PNG, CSS field/aperture and line, supplied fonts. Keep the logo colors and original geometry unchanged.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental resolves on the final logo hold with a measured closing accent; the loop returns to its opening beat. No VO/SFX requested.
