name: Account progression records
description: Product rules for achievement and lifetime collection credit
---

Only authenticated, server-authoritative completed games contribute to permanent achievements, asset ownership, retention, and history. Guest games stay local and untracked; abandoned games do not count; do not invent records for games completed before tracking existed. Preserve the existing four equipment slots, and keep Credits rewards defined but unpaid until a wallet exists.

**Why:** The progression feature is meant to be a trustworthy lifetime record without changing game rules or implying an unimplemented payout.

**How to apply:** Finalize from the authoritative match state, make completion idempotent per user and game, and verify any new achievement criterion against actual game limits and available economy features.
