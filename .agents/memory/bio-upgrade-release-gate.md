---
name: Bio Upgrade release gate
description: Why the Bio board must remain unavailable until its separate party-card flow is implemented.
---

Keep Bio Upgrade unavailable as a playable board until its own social card pool and party-choice flow are implemented. Do not let it fall back to Redline cards, whose effects change gameplay stats.

**Why:** The Bio board brief requires immediate, social-only party challenges and says Redline card behavior must remain untouched. Enabling play before the isolated Bio card system exists would expose the wrong effects.

**How to apply:** Before enabling Bio single-player or multiplayer, finish and verify the Bio-only deck composition, card presentation, target selection, CPU resolution, and synchronized multiplayer completion.
