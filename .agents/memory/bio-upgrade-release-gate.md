---
name: Bio Upgrade release gate
description: Why the Bio board must remain unavailable until its separate social cards, artwork, and party flow are ready.
---

Keep Bio Upgrade unavailable as a playable board until its own social card pool, dedicated artwork, and party-choice flow are implemented. Do not let it fall back to Redline cards, whose effects change gameplay stats.

Every Bio character must use a Bio-specific pawn variant with a color unique across the 21-character Bio roster and distinct from the standard pawn. Keep this variant wired into Bio selection and the future Bio board without changing standard pawns.

Resolve each Bio party challenge with one turn-owner action: select eligible participants, then record completion or a no-penalty pass. CPUs must auto-resolve, and pass when the match lacks enough participants; multiplayer must validate the action on the server.

**Why:** The Bio board brief requires immediate, social-only party challenges with complete artwork and says Redline card behavior must remain untouched. Enabling play before the isolated Bio experience is complete would expose missing or incorrect content.

**How to apply:** Keep party resolution atomic and non-blocking; do not require each remote participant to submit a separate vote. Before enabling Bio single-player or multiplayer, verify the Bio-only deck composition, dedicated images, target selection, CPU resolution, synchronized multiplayer completion, and unique Bio pawn colors.
