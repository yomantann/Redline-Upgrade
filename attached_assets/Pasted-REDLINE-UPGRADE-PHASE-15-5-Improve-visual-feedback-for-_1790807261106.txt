REDLINE UPGRADE — PHASE 15.5

Improve visual feedback for all existing cards, character abilities, career abilities, passive effects, and player interactions.

Do NOT change the underlying game mechanics or balance.

Every meaningful effect should clearly show:

WHO caused it → WHO was affected → WHAT happened → RESULT

Examples:

CARD RESOLVED
Rind → Executive P
- $20K Wealth

CHARACTER ABILITY
Panic Bot → Rind
-2 Influence

CAREER ABILITY
Cybersecurity / DIGITAL
Click Click
+$15K Wealth

CAREER/DECK INTERACTION
WEALTH CARD
Rind — RISK CAREER
-$10K Wealth

Requirements:

1. When a player is affected, briefly highlight their player card.

2. Show the exact change clearly:
   +$20K Wealth
   -2 Fame
   +1 AI Skill

3. Where useful, show before → after:
   Wealth: $125K → $145K

4. Clearly identify the source:
   Card, Character Ability, Career Ability, Passive Ability, Board Event, etc.

5. Clearly identify the target when another player is affected.

6. If a card/ability affects a career category or deck affinity, explicitly show WHY:
   "DIGITAL CAREER BONUS"
   "RISK CAREER EFFECT"
   etc.

7. Effects should resolve visibly rather than happening instantaneously.

8. Multiple effects should resolve sequentially so players can follow what happened.

9. Keep the presentation clean and short. Do NOT create a complicated permanent event log.

10. Make sure CPU and human effects use the same visual feedback system.

The goal is simple:

PLAYERS SHOULD NEVER HAVE TO GUESS WHETHER A CARD, CHARACTER ABILITY, CAREER ABILITY, OR PASSIVE EFFECT ACTUALLY AFFECTED SOMEONE.
