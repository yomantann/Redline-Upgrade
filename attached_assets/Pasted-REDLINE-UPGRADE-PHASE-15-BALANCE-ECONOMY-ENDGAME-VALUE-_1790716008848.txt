REDLINE UPGRADE — PHASE 15
BALANCE, ECONOMY & ENDGAME VALUE

This phase is primarily about balancing the existing game systems and making sure the three endgame choices are meaningfully different.

Do NOT add multiplayer.
Do NOT add the persistent Credits/Auth system yet.
Do NOT create a large new gameplay system.

Preserve the existing:
- Careers
- Characters
- Character abilities
- Career abilities
- Career deck affinities
- Six card decks
- Board
- 2d4 movement
- Salary system
- Salary Gates
- Milestones
- Cars
- Lifestyle
- Pets
- Investments
- Properties
- Asset upgrades
- Upgrade Tokens
- Finish line
- Finish-order rewards
- Attribute endgame bonuses
- Existing Cash Out / Double Down / Final Gamble framework

==================================================

Do not blindly rebalance everything.

Identify actual outliers first.

==================================================
2. CORE BALANCE GOAL
==================================================

The game should create meaningful decisions between:

A. Accumulating Wealth

B. Acquiring milestone assets

C. Upgrading milestone assets

D. Saving an Upgrade Token for its future persistent-credit value

E. Taking risk near the finish line

There should NOT be one universally correct strategy.

A player who focuses heavily on assets should have a meaningful endgame advantage.

A player who focuses heavily on Wealth should also have a meaningful endgame path.

A player who takes calculated risks should have a different path.

==================================================
3. STARTING WEALTH
==================================================

Starting Wealth should equal the player's current career salary.

Do not use a separate lower starting Wealth value.

Example:

Career Salary = $100K
Starting Wealth = $100K

Career Salary = $150K
Starting Wealth = $150K

Use the existing career salary value as the source of truth.

Do not duplicate salary values in separate systems.

==================================================
4. ASSETS MUST MATTER AT ENDGAME
==================================================

This is one of the most important changes in Phase 15.

Players should have a real reason to acquire milestone assets.

Players should also have a real reason to upgrade those assets.

Assets must provide some form of measurable endgame value.

Do NOT simply make every asset equivalent to a fixed amount of Wealth.

Keep the asset categories distinct.

The final system should allow assets to contribute to endgame value through a combination of:

- Wealth/value contribution
- attribute contribution
- category-specific bonuses
- level-based value
- endgame modifiers

Use the existing four attributes where appropriate:

- AI SKILL
- FAME
- LIFESTYLE
- INFLUENCE

Do not introduce additional attributes.

==================================================
5. ASSET LEVEL PROGRESSION
==================================================

Level 1 should provide meaningful value.

Level 2 should be better.

Level 3 should be better again.

Level 4 should provide the strongest endgame contribution.

The increase does NOT need to be perfectly linear.

The important thing is:

LEVEL 4 > LEVEL 3 > LEVEL 2 > LEVEL 1

in meaningful endgame value.

A player should be able to look at an asset and understand:

"I paid to upgrade this, and that will matter later."

Do not make upgrades purely cosmetic.

The existing unique Level 1–4 artwork should continue to represent the progression visually.

==================================================
6. ASSET CATEGORY IDENTITY
==================================================

Keep the categories distinct.

CAR:
Should contribute toward a mobility/status/lifestyle-oriented endgame value.

LIFESTYLE:
Should primarily contribute toward LIFESTYLE and/or Wealth-related endgame value.

PET:
Should contribute toward LIFESTYLE and potentially a secondary endgame benefit.

INVESTMENT:
Should contribute toward Wealth/economic endgame value.

PROPERTY:
Should contribute toward Wealth, LIFESTYLE, or Influence-oriented endgame value.

Do not make every category mechanically identical.

Use the existing game's attributes and Wealth system.

==================================================
7. ASSET VALUE SHOULD BE DISPLAYED
==================================================

On the player card and/or asset detail UI, make it clear that an owned asset has endgame value.

Show concise information such as:

ENDGAME VALUE
+$XXK

or:

ENDGAME
+X LIFESTYLE

or:

LEVEL 3
ENDGAME VALUE: +$XXK

Do not create a complicated accounting screen.

The player should understand why acquiring/upgrading the asset matters.

==================================================
8. THREE ENDGAME CHOICES
==================================================

Balance the three endgame choices so that each represents a different strategic decision.

The choices are:

1. CASH OUT
2. DOUBLE DOWN
3. FINAL GAMBLE

Do not make one obviously superior.

==================================================
9. CASH OUT
==================================================

CASH OUT should represent the safer endgame option.

The player locks in the value they have accumulated.

This should strongly reward:

- Wealth
- Assets
- Asset upgrades
- Consistent play

The player should not feel punished for choosing Cash Out.

It should be the reliable path.

==================================================
10. DOUBLE DOWN
==================================================

DOUBLE DOWN should represent a higher-risk, higher-reward option.

Use the existing game economy.

The player should risk something meaningful for a potentially larger endgame result.

Do not create a completely new currency or complicated secondary game.

The reward should scale appropriately with what the player has accumulated.

Assets should still matter when using Double Down.

==================================================
11. FINAL GAMBLE
==================================================

FINAL GAMBLE should be the highest-variance choice.

It should provide a legitimate possibility of a substantially better result but also carry meaningful downside.

Use the existing Gamble deck/system where possible.

Do not make it a completely separate minigame.

The result should still interact with:

- Wealth
- Assets
- Asset upgrades
- Attributes
- Finish status

==================================================
12. THREE CHOICES MUST HAVE DIFFERENT IDENTITIES
==================================================

Make the strategic identities extremely clear:

CASH OUT
"Protect what you've built."

DOUBLE DOWN
"Risk some of what you've built for more."

FINAL GAMBLE
"Go for the biggest possible outcome."

Do not use those exact descriptions if the existing UI has better terminology, but preserve the conceptual distinction.

==================================================
13. UPGRADE TOKENS — REDESIGN ENDGAME HOLDING
==================================================

Change the previous Upgrade Token endgame concept.

An Upgrade Token should primarily be valuable DURING the game because it can:

- upgrade an existing asset
OR
- recover a previously missed milestone asset

The player should therefore face a real decision:

USE TOKEN NOW
→ gain immediate asset/endgame value

OR

HOLD TOKEN
→ sacrifice that immediate opportunity for a future persistent reward

==================================================
14. HOLDING UPGRADE TOKENS
==================================================

Do NOT give held Upgrade Tokens a direct Wealth bonus at the end of the match.

Instead:

Upgrade Tokens held through the end of the game should eventually contribute to the future persistent Credits system.

The persistent Credits system is NOT being implemented in Phase 15.

For now:

- Track how many Upgrade Tokens the player finishes with.
- Preserve that value in the final game result/state.
- Clearly identify it as a future Credits conversion value.
- Do not invent the actual Credits currency yet.

This creates a meaningful sacrifice:

USE TOKEN
→ improve your current game

HOLD TOKEN
→ give up the immediate upgrade opportunity in exchange for greater future meta progression

==================================================
15. UPGRADE TOKEN VALUE
==================================================

Do not make Upgrade Tokens so common that players can freely upgrade everything.

Do not make them so rare that the system becomes irrelevant.

Audit:

- board Upgrade Token spaces
- card-based Upgrade Token rewards
- career starting Upgrade Token
- other existing sources

Report expected average Upgrade Tokens per player.

Use the current intended availability unless the audit identifies a serious balance issue.

==================================================
16. MISSED MILESTONE RECOVERY
==================================================

Preserve the existing rule:

If a player uses an Upgrade Token to recover a missed milestone:

- They must have already passed that milestone.
- They do NOT choose which missed asset to recover.
- The recovered milestone asset is randomly assigned from the applicable missed milestone options.
- The player receives it for free.

Do not allow players to strategically choose the strongest missed asset.

==================================================
17. ENDGAME ASSET CALCULATION
==================================================

Create a centralized endgame calculation.

Calculate:

FINAL WEALTH
+
ASSET ENDGAME VALUE
+
FINISH-ORDER REWARD
+
ATTRIBUTE BONUSES
+
OTHER EXISTING ENDGAME MODIFIERS
=
ENDGAME TOTAL

Then apply the selected:

CASH OUT
DOUBLE DOWN
FINAL GAMBLE

result.

Do not scatter endgame calculations across multiple unrelated reducers/components.

Create one clear source of truth.

==================================================
18. ASSET + ENDGAME INTERACTION
==================================================

The system should ensure that acquiring assets earlier provides a meaningful advantage.

Example progression:

Player A:
More Wealth
Few assets

Player B:
Moderate Wealth
Several Level 2–3 assets

Player C:
Lower Wealth
Fewer but highly upgraded assets

All three should have legitimate paths to a strong finish.

Do NOT force assets to dominate Wealth.

Do NOT make Wealth automatically dominate assets.

==================================================
19. FINISH ORDER
==================================================

Preserve the existing finish-order reward system.

The player who reaches the finish first should receive an additional reward.

The reward should be meaningful but should not completely overwhelm:

- Wealth
- Assets
- Upgrades
- Attribute bonuses

Audit the current values and adjust only if necessary.

Show finish order clearly on the player card/board.

==================================================
20. FOUR ATTRIBUTE ENDGAME BONUSES
==================================================

Preserve:

MOST AI SKILL
+$50K

MOST FAME
+$50K

MOST LIFESTYLE
+$50K

MOST INFLUENCE
+$50K

These are awarded when the final player crosses the finish.

Ensure asset contributions can meaningfully influence these attributes where appropriate.

Do not allow an asset strategy to make the attribute bonuses irrelevant.

==================================================
21. BALANCE TARGETS
==================================================

Do not target perfect mathematical equality.

Target:

- multiple viable strategies
- meaningful tradeoffs
- no obviously dominant asset category
- no career that completely overwhelms others
- no deck that dominates the economy
- upgrades should feel worth considering
- Cash Out should feel safe
- Double Down should feel risky but attractive
- Final Gamble should feel genuinely high variance
- holding Upgrade Tokens should have a meaningful opportunity cost
- assets should matter at the finish

==================================================
22. DO NOT OVER-COMPLICATE THE UI
==================================================

Do NOT introduce complicated financial breakdowns.

The player should see concise values.

Examples:

ASSET ENDGAME
+$75K

LEVEL 3
+$125K ENDGAME

MOST FAME
+$50K

FINISH #2
+$25K

UPGRADE TOKEN
1 HELD

Keep detailed calculations available internally but don't overwhelm the main gameplay UI.
