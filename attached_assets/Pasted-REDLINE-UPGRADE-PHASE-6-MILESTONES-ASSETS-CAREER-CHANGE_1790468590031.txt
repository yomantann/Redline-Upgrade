REDLINE UPGRADE — PHASE 6
MILESTONES, ASSETS & CAREER CHANGE

Phase 5 is complete.

Now build the first major milestone/asset system.

IMPORTANT:
Keep this phase focused. Do not build multiplayer, endgame, gambling cards, upgrades, or authentication yet.

==================================================
1. MILESTONE STRUCTURE
==================================================

Use these milestone locations:

SPACE 10 — CAR
SPACE 30 — LIFESTYLE
SPACE 35 — CAREER CHANGE
SPACE 45 — PET OR INVESTMENT
SPACE 60 — PROPERTY
SPACE 75 — FINAL ENDGAME PLACEHOLDER

Milestone spaces trigger when LANDING on them.

EXCEPTION:
Space 35 Career Change triggers when the player LANDs ON or PASSES THROUGH it.

Salary Gates continue to trigger on landing OR passing.

==================================================
2. MILESTONE UI
==================================================

When a player reaches a milestone, pause their turn and display a dedicated choice screen.

Choices should be presented as large, visual board-game-style cards.

Every card must clearly show:

- Image/visual
- Name
- Category
- Cost
- Stat effects
- Passive/special effect if applicable
- BUY/SELECT button

Do not use tiny buttons or simple text lists.

The player should immediately understand:

WHAT AM I GETTING?
WHAT DOES IT COST?
WHAT DOES IT DO?

5. SPACE 35 — CAREER CHANGE
==================================================

Space 35 is NOT a normal milestone.

Create a dedicated CAREER_CHANGE board-space type.

It triggers when the player:

- Lands on Space 35
OR
- Passes through Space 35

It should trigger exactly once during a movement.

When triggered, pause the turn.

Display:

CAREER OPPORTUNITY

Show the player's current career.

Give the player TWO choices:

KEEP CAREER
SWITCH CAREER

--------------------------------
KEEP CAREER
--------------------------------

If selected:

- Keep current career
- Keep career category
- Keep career ability
- Keep current salary
- Keep current salary tier
- Keep existing Wealth

Show:

CAREER LOCKED IN

Then resume the game.

--------------------------------
SWITCH CAREER
--------------------------------

If selected:

Reveal TWO random career cards.

The current career cannot be one of the choices.

The two choices must be different.

Each card shows:

- Career name
- Career icon
- Career category
- Salary range
- Career ability
- Description

Example:

AI ENGINEER
DIGITAL / RISK
$70K–$200K
MODEL UPGRADE

DEGEN TRADER
DIGITAL / RISK
$20K–$500K
YOLO

The player chooses one.

--------------------------------
NEW SALARY
--------------------------------

After selecting the new career:

Randomly assign one of that career's four salary tiers.

Use the existing LOW / STANDARD / HIGH / ELITE salary system.

Show a visual salary reveal.

Example:

NEW CAREER

DEGEN TRADER

SALARY TIER

ELITE

$500K

The salary must come from the selected career's own salary table.

Do NOT give the player additional starting Wealth when changing careers.

Existing Wealth remains unchanged.

Future Salary Gates use the new salary.

--------------------------------
PLAYER CARD UPDATE
--------------------------------

If the player switches careers, update:

- Career
- Career icon
- Career category
- Career ability
- Salary
- Salary tier

The old career is replaced.

11. CPU MILESTONE DECISIONS
==================================================

CPU players should automatically make milestone decisions.

Use simple logic.

Race Driver:
Prefer Car.

Real Estate Investor:
Prefer Property/Investment.

Degen Trader:
Prefer Investment.

Influencer/Content Creator/Entertainer:
Prefer Fame/Lifestyle.

AI Engineer/Cybersecurity:
May prefer AI-related assets.

At Space 35:

CPU first decides:

KEEP or SWITCH

If SWITCH:

1. Generate two random eligible careers.
2. Choose one using simple logic plus some randomness.
3. Assign a random salary tier from the selected career.
4. Update the CPU Player Card.

Do not build advanced AI.
