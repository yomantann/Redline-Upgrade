# REDLINE UPGRADE — PHASE 5

## CAREERS + SALARY + CAREER CATEGORIES + WEALTH

Phase 4.5 is complete.

We now have:

* 75-space 3D tabletop board
* Zoomable/pannable board
* 1 Human + 3 CPU players
* 3D character pieces
* 2d4 dice
* Animated dice rolls
* Turn system
* Movement system
* Landing-space system

Now build the player's career, salary, career-category, and Wealth foundation.

Keep this phase data-driven and modular.

---

# 1. BOARD CAMERA

Before beginning the career system, confirm the board supports:

* Zoom IN
* Zoom OUT
* Pan around the board
* Reset/center camera

Use smooth camera movement.

The player should be able to zoom in enough to see their 3D character pieces and individual spaces clearly.

They should also be able to zoom out enough to understand the entire 75-space route.

Do not allow zooming so far in/out that the board becomes unusable.

# 10. STARTING WEALTH

Do not create another money resource.

The only financial resource is:

**WEALTH**

Starting Wealth should be determined by the player's salary.

Use a configurable formula.

Example:

`Starting Wealth = Salary × Starting Wealth Multiplier`

The multiplier should be easy to rebalance.

Career-specific modifiers can be supported.

---

# 11. PAYDAY SYSTEM

This is an important core mechanic.

Certain board spaces should be designated:

**PAYDAY**

A Payday space pays the player's **current salary** into their Wealth.

### IMPORTANT:

Payday triggers when the player:

**LANDS ON**

OR

**PASSES THROUGH**

a Payday space.

Example:

Player has:

`$140K Salary`

They move from Space 8 to Space 15.

Space 12 is a Payday.

The player receives:

`+$140K Wealth`

Even though they did not land on Space 12.

If they land directly on Space 12:

They ALSO receive:

`+$140K Wealth`

Do not accidentally pay twice for the same Payday space during a single movement.

Each Payday space crossed during a single move should trigger once.

---

# 12. PAYDAY SPACES

Add Payday as a supported board-space property.

A space can have:

* NORMAL
* EVENT
* MILESTONE
* GAMBLE
* PAYDAY

Payday can eventually coexist with another space type if useful.

For example:

`PAYDAY + EVENT`

But keep the initial implementation simple.

Spread several Payday spaces throughout the 75-space board.

Do not manually hardcode the logic into movement.

The movement system should detect:

**Did the player cross a Payday space?**

If yes:

`player.wealth += player.salary`

**"I just collected my salary."**

This should work for:

* Human

---

# 14. PASSING VS LANDING

The movement system must distinguish:

### PASSING

The player moves through each intermediate space.

Any PASS effects trigger.

### LANDING

After movement finishes, process the final space's LAND effects.

Example:

Player rolls 6:

`10 → 11 → 12 → 13 → 14 → 15 → 16`

If Space 12 is Payday:

Pay salary.

If Space 16 is an Event:

Process Space 16 after movement ends.

This distinction will become extremely important later.

# 20. IMPORTANT ECONOMY RULE

Do NOT automatically give players their salary every turn.

Salary is only collected when a player:

**LANDS ON or PASSES THROUGH a Payday space.**

This makes salary an important part of the board progression.

A high-salary career is valuable because every Payday becomes more valuable.

A low-salary career may have other advantages through abilities, risk, gambling, or future upgrades.

# SUCCESS CRITERIA

I should be able to:

1. Start a game.
2. Select my character.
3. Receive a random career.
4. See the career category and icon.
5. Receive one of four salary tiers.
6. Clearly see whether I received LOW, STANDARD, HIGH, or ELITE salary.
7. Receive starting Wealth.
8. See all of this on my player HUD.
9. See the same information for the 3 CPU players.
10. Roll the existing 2d4.
11. Move across the board.
12. Pass through a Payday space.
13. Automatically receive my full current salary as Wealth.
14. Land directly on a Payday space and receive my salary.
15. Never receive the same Payday reward twice during one movement.
16. See an animated `+$Salary` Wealth notification.
17. Continue playing normally.
18. Have the underlying system ready for future career-specific and career-category events.

The fundamental economic loop should now be:

**CAREER**
↓
**SALARY**
↓
**PAYDAY**
↓
**WEALTH**
↓
**FUTURE ASSETS / GAMBLING / UPGRADES / ENDGAME**

Keep the implementation modular and easy to rebalance.
