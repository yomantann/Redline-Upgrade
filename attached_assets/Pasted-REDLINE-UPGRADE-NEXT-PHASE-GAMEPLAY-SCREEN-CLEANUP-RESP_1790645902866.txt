==================================================
1. PC PLAYER CARDS — EXPANDED ABOVE BOARD
==================================================

On desktop/PC, restore the player/character cards to the expanded presentation used previously.

Player cards should appear ABOVE the board.

They should be visible without requiring the player to open them.

Show useful information including:

- Character
- Career
- Salary
- Salary tier
- Wealth
- Core attributes
- Upgrade Tokens
- Career deck affinities
- Character ability
- Career ability
- Owned milestone assets
- Finish status/order when applicable

Keep the design clean and color coded.

Do not make the cards unnecessarily tall.

The player should be able to understand the current players before looking at the board.

==================================================
2. PHONE PLAYER CARDS — MINIMIZED BY DEFAULT
==================================================

On phone/mobile:

Player cards should still appear ABOVE the board.

However, they should start MINIMIZED.

Collapsed view should show a compact summary:

- Character
- Career
- Wealth
- Salary
- Upgrade Tokens

Allow the player to expand the card when they want detailed information.

Do not force mobile players to scroll through large expanded cards before reaching the board.

4. SIX DECK DISPLAY POSITION
==================================================

The six deck/card visual area is currently being pushed too far down on PC.

Fix the layout.
