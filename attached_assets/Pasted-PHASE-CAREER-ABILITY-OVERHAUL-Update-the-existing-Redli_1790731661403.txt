Update the existing Redline Upgrade career ability system.

IMPORTANT:
This phase is specifically about REDOING SELECTED CAREER ABILITIES and adding the two new careers THIEF and ALIEN.

DO NOT change:
- Existing board layout
- Character abilities
- Card deck mechanics
- Milestone mechanics
- Upgrade Token mechanics
- Endgame mechanics
- Existing career categories unless specifically required for the two new careers

Only change career abilities and the supporting event/UI logic required for these abilities.

==================================================
1. CAREER CHANGE VS CAREER SWAP — CRITICAL
==================================================

There are TWO completely different mechanics.

CAREER CHANGE:

When a player voluntarily changes careers through the existing Space 35 Career Change system:

1. Player chooses to stay OR switch.
2. If switching, show the existing 2 random career choices.
3. Player selects a new career.
4. Generate a NEW RANDOM SALARY appropriate to that new career.

The old salary is replaced.

CAREER SWAP:

When a career ability/card causes two players to swap careers:

- Swap Career
- Swap Career ability
- Swap Career categories
- Swap Primary deck affinity
- Swap Secondary deck affinity
- Swap Salary

The salary stays attached to the career being transferred.

Example:

Player A:
Lawyer
$100K salary

Player B:
Corporate Executive
$75K salary

After Career Swap:

Player A:
Corporate Executive
$75K salary

Player B:
Lawyer
$100K salary

DO NOT reroll salaries during a career swap.

Career swap does NOT transfer:

- Character
- Wealth
- AI Skill
- Fame
- Lifestyle
- Influence
- Assets
- Upgrade Tokens
- Finish position
- Other player-specific progression

Use ONE shared career-swap operation for every career-swap mechanic.

==================================================
2. DOCTOR — UPGRADE TOKEN
==================================================

Doctor's unique distinction is now ONLY:

STARTS WITH 1 UPGRADE TOKEN.

Remove the existing Doctor "block one Lifestyle or AI Skill penalty" ability.

IMPORTANT:

Whenever a player obtains the Doctor career, they receive Doctor's 1 Upgrade Token.

This applies if they:

- Start as Doctor
- Select Doctor through Space 35
- Obtain Doctor through a career swap
- Obtain Doctor through another valid future career-acquisition mechanic

The token should be granted once when Doctor is acquired.

If Doctor is obtained through a CAREER SWAP, the Doctor career package includes the Doctor Upgrade Token.

Do not reroll the salary during the swap.

Do not repeatedly grant additional Doctor tokens simply because the player still has Doctor.

Do not remove an already-earned Upgrade Token if the player later changes careers.
