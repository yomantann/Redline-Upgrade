==================================================
CHARACTER ABILITY DESIGN
==================================================

The characters should feel genuinely different.

Examples of the TYPE of abilities we want:

LAND ON PLAYER:
- steal a small amount of Wealth
- exchange a stat
- gain Influence
- trigger a character-specific interaction

OTHER PLAYER LANDS ON YOU:
- receive a benefit
- protect yourself
- gain Wealth/Influence/Fame
- trigger a reaction

ROLL 8:
- gain Wealth
- gain Fame
- gain AI Skill
- trigger another limited effect

ROLL DOUBLE:
- gain a bonus
- modify the result of an event
- trigger a special character effect

ANOTHER PLAYER ROLLS DOUBLE:
- react to their roll
- gain a small benefit
- affect that player
- gain protection

PASS PLAYER:
- gain a benefit
- affect the player passed

PASS PAYDAY:
- receive an additional bonus

DRAW CARD:
- modify a card outcome
- gain a bonus from a specific deck
- protect against a negative effect

PURCHASE:
- receive a bonus from a Car/Property/Lifestyle/Investment purchase

These are examples of mechanics.

Do NOT assign the same type of ability to every character.

Use the existing character identity, name, artwork, and theme when designing abilities.

==================================================
CHARACTER BALANCE

Mix:

- passive abilities

==================================================
CAREER/DECK INTERACTION
==================================================

Career affinity should matter when the player draws from a matching deck.

Examples:

A matching career may:

- gain an additional small benefit
- receive a modified card outcome
- gain protection from a negative card
- gain a bonus stat
- trigger a career-specific effect

Do NOT make every matching card dramatically stronger.

The affinity should feel like a useful career advantage, not an automatic win.
