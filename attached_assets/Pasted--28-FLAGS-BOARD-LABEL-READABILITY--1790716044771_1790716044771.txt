- Major board areas

==================================================

Remove the large preview-card presentation from the normal board layout.

PC:
Place the deck cards/deck area in a compact presentation UNDERNEATH THE DICE / YOUR THROW area.

Phone:

30. BOARD DRESSING — MORE UNIQUE 3D STRUCTURES
==================================================

The current structures around the sides of the board are too much like generic houses.

Replace the generic house-heavy dressing with more unique futuristic structures that feel specific to the Redline Upgrade world.

Examples of the type of visual direction:

- futuristic towers
- unusual commercial structures
- futuristic garages
- industrial structures
- luxury buildings
- neon architectural installations
- unusual entertainment venues
- futuristic roadside structures
- large signs integrated into architecture
- distinctive silhouettes

Do NOT simply create larger houses.

The structures should feel like miniature environmental scenery surrounding the board.

They may be approximately TWICE the current scale if necessary to create stronger visual presence.

However:

- Do not obstruct board spaces
- Do not cover player pieces
- Do not interfere with movement
- Do not obscure text
- Do not make the board feel cluttered

The structures should make the board feel like a miniature world rather than a flat game UI.

==================================================
31. BOARD STRUCTURE FALLBACK
==================================================

If creating appropriate 3D structures in the current environment is producing low-quality or generic results, DO NOT fill the board with poor 3D objects just to satisfy the requirement.

A clean 2D environmental solution is acceptable.

In that case, use high-quality transparent PNG environmental artwork positioned around the board corners/perimeter.

The PNGs should:

- have transparent backgrounds
- have strong silhouettes
- look like part of the same world
- remain behind/outside the playable board
- not interfere with spaces or player pieces

Do not use generic placeholder shapes.

The priority is:

GOOD 2D ENVIRONMENTAL ART
over
BAD 3D STRUCTURES.
